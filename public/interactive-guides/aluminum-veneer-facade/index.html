Build-Up Sequence

The eight site operations, with the tools, materials, method and acceptance check for each. The interactive viewer beside this list plays the same sequence on a to-scale model.

  1. 1Survey & Setting Out

    As-built wall survey + panel module grid on the wall

    Duration 1 day / 2 people

    Tools Laser level / plumb laser · 5 m tape · Chalk line · Rebar / service detector

    Method

    1. Check the wall is solid and can take anchors; detect and mark rebar and services.
    2. Survey the as-built face (flatness, plumb) and record deviations.
    3. Establish the façade face datum and snap the vertical / horizontal module grid on the wall, tied to the building control lines.
    4. Snap every panel joint centreline — the grid tubes will be fixed on these same lines, so the panel ears land on a tube.

    Acceptance

    • Wall sound, services located and avoided
    • Grid square, setting-out deviation ≤ 2 mm
    • No edge panel narrower than 1/3 of a full panel

    The tube grid and the panel ears share these lines — setting out here fixes both where the anchors go and that every ear lands on a tube.

  2. 2Fix Steel Grid to Wall

    Expansion anchors on the grid lines, then the RHS tube mesh

    Duration 2–3 days / team

    Tools Hammer drill · Torque wrench · Plumb laser · 2 m straight edge · Weld / bolt set

    Materials M10 expansion anchor bolts · RHS 40×40×3 galvanised tubes · Insulating pads

    Method

    1. Drill through the tube back wall into the concrete on the marked crossings, clean the holes and set the expansion anchors — one bolt per crossing, its hex head bearing on the tube back.
    2. Erect the vertical and horizontal tubes on the anchors, the mesh matching the panel module exactly (tubes on the joint / edge centrelines).
    3. Plumb and level each run, true the whole face, then lock the grid crossings (bolted or welded).
    4. Touch up galvanising breaks with zinc-rich paint.

    Acceptance

    • Anchors no movement by hand; torque verified
    • Mesh on module and on the datum plane; face straight, no wave
    • Every panel ear position has a tube behind it

    This is the load path and the setting-out in one layer — the wall tolerance is taken up at the anchors, never at the panel face.

  3. 3Place Panel 1

    Set panel 1 on the grid so its 8 integral ears lie flat on the tubes

    Duration per panel, team + suction cups

    Tools Suction cups / hoist · Level · Shims · Plumb laser

    Materials Aluminium veneer panel (ears bent on in the shop)

    Method

    1. Take the first panel from the fixed corner; it sets the datum for the whole group.
    2. Bring it to the grid so all 8 integral ears sit flat on the tubes behind — no gap under any ear.
    3. Set its level, joint width and face flush; hold with shims. Do not screw yet — the next step drives the screws.

    Acceptance

    • Panel 1 resting on the grid with all 8 ears flat on the tubes
    • Level, joint width and face set against the setting-out lines
    • Held securely, not yet fixed

    Placing and screwing are separate steps on purpose: first you see the panel land on its ears, then the screws that hold it go in.

  4. 4Screw Panel 1

    Drive 8 self-tapping screws through panel 1's ears into the tubes

    Duration per panel, drill-driver

    Tools Drill-driver / torque driver · Magnetic bit · Safety glasses

    Materials 8 self-tapping screws to steel (stainless / coated)

    Method

    1. With panel 1 held on the grid, drive one self-tapping screw through each of the 8 ear holes into the steel tube behind.
    2. Screws go perpendicular to the panel face — this is what actually fixes the panel to the subframe.
    3. Re-check flush and joint width after screwing, then release the suction cups and move to the next panel.

    Acceptance

    • All 8 screws seated, panel does not rock, ear tabs flat on the tubes

    Nothing is screwed through the visible face — the panel is held only by these 8 ear screws, so any single panel can come back off.

  11. 11Seal the Joints

    After the group is hung: backer rod + weatherseal

    Duration After panel alignment

    Tools Cleaning cloths · Backer-rod wheel · Caulking gun · Masking tape · Tooling bead

    Materials Closed-cell backer rod · Neutral-cure silicone weatherseal

    Method

    1. Once all four panels are hung and screwed and the joints are even, clean both bond faces and let them dry; mask both edges.
    2. Set the backer rod to depth so the sealant is about half the joint width.
    3. Apply and tool the sealant to a smooth concave bead before it skins; remove masking while wet.

    Acceptance

    • Bead continuous, straight, evenly tooled
    • No voids, no three-sided bond
    • No smearing on the panel face

    Sealing is done after the panels are set as a group, so the joint width is already even and the bead comes out straight.

  12. 12Inspection & Handover

    Peel film, final line-and-level, record

    Duration 0.5 day / team

    Tools Straight edge · Tape · Visual inspection kit

    Method

    1. Peel the protection film, clean the faces and inspect for scratches, dents and colour difference.
    2. Re-check joint width, face flatness and the overall line against the setting-out drawing.
    3. Verify every ear screw and anchor is tightened; touch-up any coating breaks.
    4. Record the as-built and hand over the access / replacement panel schedule.

    Acceptance

    • Face flat, joints even, no visible defect
    • All fixings tightened and marked
    • As-built record and spares handed over

    A single-panel-demountable façade only works if the fixing record and spare panels are handed over with it.
